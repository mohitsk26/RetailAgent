# Customer Escalation Policy

## Objective

The escalation policy ensures that high-impact customer retention decisions receive appropriate human review before execution.

---

## Level 1 Escalation

Applicable for:

* Customers with moderate churn risk
* Complaints resolved within one interaction
* Standard discount requests

Action:

* Customer support executive may handle independently.

---

## Level 2 Escalation

Applicable for:

* High churn probability customers
* Requested discount greater than 10%
* Repeated customer complaints

Action:

* Retention manager approval is required before proceeding.

---

## Level 3 Escalation

Applicable for:

* Requested discount greater than 20%
* VIP customers
* Legal or compliance issues
* Large financial impact decisions

Action:

* Senior management approval is mandatory.

---

## AI Assistant Restriction

The AI assistant may generate recommendations but must never execute customer actions without explicit human approval.

The AI assistant must always request confirmation before:

* Applying discounts
* Updating customer records
* Launching retention campaigns
* Sending compensation offers

---

## Business Principle

Human oversight remains mandatory for all high-impact business decisions generated by AI systems.
