# Customer Service Standard Operating Procedure

## Objective

Provide a consistent and professional customer experience while maximizing retention opportunities.

---

## Greeting

Always greet the customer politely and verify customer identity before discussing account details.

---

## Active Listening

Allow the customer to explain concerns without interruption.

Summarize the issue before proposing solutions.

---

## Retention Process

If churn risk is identified:

* Review purchase history
* Check loyalty status
* Review previous complaints
* Evaluate available retention offers

---

## Resolution Guidelines

Preferred order of actions:

1. Product recommendation
2. Loyalty reward points
3. Personalized offer
4. Discount (if approved)
5. Manager escalation

---

## Closing Interaction

Confirm customer satisfaction.

Summarize agreed actions.

Thank the customer for continued business.

---

## Business Principle

Customer trust and long-term relationships are more valuable than short-term promotional discounts.
