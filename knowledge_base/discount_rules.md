# Discount Approval Policy

## Objective

The objective of this policy is to provide controlled customer retention discounts while protecting company profitability.

---

## Automatic Discount Approval

The AI system may recommend discounts up to 10%.

No manual approval is required.

---

## Manager Approval Required

Discounts greater than 10% and up to 20% require approval from the retention manager before being offered to the customer.

---

## Senior Management Approval Required

Discounts greater than 20% require approval from senior management.

The AI assistant must never automatically authorize these discounts.

---

## Agent Restrictions

The AI system may generate recommendations but must not execute customer-facing actions without explicit human approval.

The AI assistant must always request confirmation before applying discounts, modifying customer records, or initiating retention campaigns.

---

## Business Principle

Human decision-makers remain responsible for final approval of high-impact customer retention actions.
