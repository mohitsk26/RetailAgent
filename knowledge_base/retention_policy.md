# Customer Retention Policy

## Objective

The objective of this policy is to reduce customer churn by providing personalized retention strategies based on customer behavior and risk level.

---

## High Churn Risk Customers

Criteria:

* High predicted churn probability
* Long purchase inactivity
* Low purchase frequency
* Low spending behavior

Recommended actions:

* Offer a personalized discount between 10% and 15%.
* Provide loyalty reward points.
* Send personalized email campaigns.
* Schedule a customer care follow-up call.

---

## Medium Churn Risk Customers

Recommended actions:

* Recommend products based on previous purchases.
* Provide limited-time promotional offers.
* Send engagement notifications.

---

## Low Churn Risk Customers

Recommended actions:

* Continue regular engagement.
* Introduce loyalty benefits.
* Promote premium membership plans.

---

## Business Principle

Retention offers should maximize long-term customer value rather than short-term sales.
