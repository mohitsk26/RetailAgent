# Loyalty Program Policy

## Objective

The loyalty program rewards customers for continued engagement and long-term purchasing behavior.

---

## Silver Tier

Benefits:

* Standard promotional offers
* Access to seasonal sales
* Basic customer support

Upgrade Criteria:

* Total spending exceeds ₹100,000
* Minimum 10 completed purchases

---

## Gold Tier

Benefits:

* Priority customer support
* Early access to promotions
* Bonus reward points
* Personalized recommendations

Upgrade Criteria:

* Total spending exceeds ₹300,000
* Minimum 25 completed purchases

---

## Platinum Tier

Benefits:

* Dedicated relationship manager
* Exclusive product launches
* Premium support
* Highest reward multiplier

Eligibility:

* Total spending exceeds ₹750,000
* Minimum 50 completed purchases

---

## Retention Strategy

Customers with increasing purchase frequency should be encouraged toward the next loyalty tier to improve long-term retention and customer lifetime value.

---

## Business Principle

Loyalty programs should increase customer engagement while maintaining sustainable business profitability.
