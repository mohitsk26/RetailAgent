CUSTOMER RETENTION AND LOYALTY POLICY

Company:
RetailMart Pvt Ltd

Version:
2.1

Effective Date:
January 2026

--------------------------------------------------

OBJECTIVE

The objective of this policy is to reduce customer churn by providing personalized engagement strategies based on customer purchasing behavior and loyalty status.

--------------------------------------------------

HIGH CHURN RISK CUSTOMERS

A customer is considered high churn risk if:

- No purchases in the last 90 days
- Purchase frequency less than 5 orders
- Total spending below $15,000
- Multiple unresolved complaints

Recommended retention strategy:

- Provide a personalized discount between 10% and 15%
- Award 1000 loyalty reward points
- Send a personalized email campaign
- Schedule a customer success follow-up call within 48 hours
- Recommend products from previous purchase history

--------------------------------------------------

MEDIUM CHURN RISK CUSTOMERS

Criteria:

- No purchase between 45 and 90 days
- Purchase frequency between 5 and 10 orders

Recommended actions:

- Offer limited-time promotional discounts
- Send push notifications
- Recommend trending products
- Invite customer to loyalty program

--------------------------------------------------

LOW CHURN RISK CUSTOMERS

Criteria:

- Purchased within last 30 days
- Frequent buyer
- High engagement score

Recommended actions:

- Continue regular engagement
- Offer premium membership
- Give early access to sales
- Reward loyalty milestones

--------------------------------------------------

CUSTOMER SERVICE SOP

Every customer interaction should follow:

1. Verify customer identity.

2. Listen actively.

3. Review purchase history.

4. Review complaint history.

5. Check loyalty status.

6. Apply retention policy.

7. Escalate to manager if required.

--------------------------------------------------

REFUND POLICY

Customers can request refunds within 30 days of purchase.

Products must be unused and returned with original packaging.

Refunds are processed within 5 business days after inspection.

--------------------------------------------------

RETURN POLICY

Damaged products may be returned within 15 days.

Defective products qualify for replacement or full refund.

Customers should upload purchase invoice before return approval.

--------------------------------------------------

LOYALTY PROGRAM

Silver Customers

- Earn 1 point per dollar spent.

Gold Customers

- Earn 2 points per dollar spent.

- Free delivery.

Platinum Customers

- Earn 3 points per dollar spent.

- Free delivery.

- Priority customer support.

- Exclusive promotional offers.

--------------------------------------------------

RECOMMENDATION ENGINE RULES

Fashion customers should receive personalized apparel recommendations.

Electronics customers should receive warranty extension offers.

Beauty customers should receive bundle recommendations.

Sports customers should receive seasonal promotions.

Home customers should receive furniture and decor bundles.

--------------------------------------------------

BUSINESS PRINCIPLE

Customer retention should maximize long-term customer lifetime value rather than short-term revenue.

Retention campaigns should be personalized and data-driven.
